YHOGA KG REFINEMENT PLAN — WHAT I'M DOING & WHY
=================================================
Author: Soros
Date: 2026-05-08
Audience: lab-mates / thesis friends


TL;DR
-----
I'm switching to Yhoga's ontology as canonical for our shared KG-completion
work. Before I run the cross-document completion step (Step 2), I'm doing a
one-time refinement pass on Yhoga's KG using my own KG as a reference.

The goal: make Yhoga's graph capture as much of the textbook content as
possible, so that when the LLM-driven completion step runs, the inputs are
clean and the cross-disciplinary links it discovers are real (not artifacts
of missing nodes or duplicate stubs).


THE CONTEXT
-----------
We have two KGs over the same three textbooks (Biologi / Fisika / Kimia,
Kelas XII Kurikulum Merdeka):

- My KG (Soros)  : 511 Konsep, 17 Bab. Schema is Konsep/SubKonsep style.
- Yhoga's KG     : 438 Concept + 181 ConceptTarget, 17 Chapter.
                   Schema is Concept/Subtopic/Chapter/Grade style — the
                   one defined in docs/yhoga-ontology.ttl.

Chapter coverage matches 1:1 across both KGs (same source PDFs).

We're going with Yhoga's ontology because it has:
  - A cleaner relation taxonomy (8 within-book + 5 cross-book LINTAS_BUKU_*).
  - First-class cross-disciplinary relations (LINTAS_BUKU_SAMA_DENGAN,
    LINTAS_BUKU_APLIKASI_DARI, etc.) — which is what our thesis is about.
  - A `ConceptTarget` mechanism for forward-references that makes
    incremental extraction tractable.


WHAT I FOUND COMPARING THE TWO KGs
----------------------------------
Of my 511 Konsep names, when matched (case-insensitive) against Yhoga:

  162 (32%)  → already in Yhoga as Concept     (overlap, no action)
   37 (7%)   → in Yhoga only as ConceptTarget  (HIGH-VALUE drop-in upgrade)
  312 (61%)  → not in Yhoga at all             (mix of real gaps and
                                                near-duplicates)

The 37 ConceptTarget hits are the cleanest win: Yhoga's extractor already
knows these concepts exist (they're referenced as relation targets in the
graph) but never extracted full definitions. My pipeline already has the
descriptions, formulas, and conditions for them.

----------------------------------------------------------
Now the similarity search has high-quality nodes to compare across

that emits the LINTAS_BUKU_* relations gets clean inputs.


WHY THIS MATTERS FOR THE THESIS
-------------------------------
Two reasons.

1. Validity of the completion step.
   If the input graph is half-stubs and case-duplicates, the cosine
   similarity will surface fake matches (lowercase "energi" pseudo-matching
   real "Energi", junk strings clustering with random Concepts). The
   completion step's precision/recall numbers become uninterpretable.

2. Cross-disciplinary signal.
   The whole point of LINTAS_BUKU_* relations is to discover that
   "Difusi" in Biologi corresponds to "Gerak Brown" in Fisika, etc.
   That only works if BOTH sides have the concepts present as proper
   Concept nodes with descriptions. Promoting Yhoga's 37 ConceptTargets
   directly increases the number of valid bridge candidates.
